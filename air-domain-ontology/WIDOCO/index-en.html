Air Domain Ontology: Description back to ToC

A comprehensive ontology of the civil air transport domain, covering air passenger and cargo/freight operations, airport infrastructure and operations, aircraft and airworthiness, flight operations and crew, air traffic management, ground handling, regulatory/certification instruments, safety and security, and environmental sustainability. Consolidated from the project's existing class/relationship tables (Air_Freight_and_Passenger_Ontology, Sheet3 organisational/regulatory layers, the cargo-handling 'Category' breakdown, and the Airport Operations Plan / APOC concepts) and extended with generally-recognised air-domain concepts not yet captured there. No upper ontology (e.g. BFO) is imported; a small set of pragmatic top-level classes (Agent, PhysicalObject, InformationObject, Process, Location, PolicyOrRegulation, Measurement, Service, Category) organises the hierarchy instead.
